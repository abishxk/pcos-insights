About the data and model

What this tool is built on, how it was trained, and what it can and cannot tell you.

What is PCOS

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a disorder involving infrequent, irregular or prolonged menstrual periods, and often excess male hormone (androgen) levels. The ovaries develop numerous small collections of fluid, called follicles, and may fail to regularly release eggs.

The dataset

{{ meta.n_samples }} patient records collected from 10 different hospitals across Kerala, India, covering the physical and clinical parameters used to determine PCOS and related infertility issues. Source file: PCOS_data_without_infertility.xlsx. {{ base_rate }}% of the recorded patients had PCOS.

This app's self-check uses a 13-field subset of that data, limited to answers a person can report themselves, without a scan or blood work.

The model

K-Nearest Neighbours, looking at the {{ meta.best_params.n_neighbors }} most similar recorded patients to produce an estimate. It is a similarity match, not a rule-based diagnosis. On a held-out test split it reached {{ (meta.metrics.test_accuracy * 100) | round | int }}% accuracy, ROC-AUC {{ meta.metrics.test_roc_auc }}.

The predictor

The self-check on this site turns your answers into the same 13 fields the model was trained on, finds the most similar patients in the data, and reports what share of them had PCOS. It is a screening estimate built from patterns in one dataset, not a clinical assessment of you.

Disclaimers
  • This tool is for general education only and is not a medical device.
  • It does not diagnose PCOS or any other condition, and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, examination, imaging or blood work.
  • Always consult a qualified doctor about your symptoms, test results and treatment.
  • Estimates are based on patterns in one published dataset and may not reflect your individual situation.
  • Your answers are used only to compute your result and are not stored anywhere.
